The home screen

Features

Made for the remote.
Clear to look at.

01

Clear glass dock

Your favorite apps sit on a frosted glass bar. The focused app grows and shows its name.

02

Your own background

Choose a preset or your own picture. The glass reflects whatever you pick.

03

Now playing

A small player at the top shows the song, progress and playback buttons.

04

Day and date

Large, spaced lettering that adapts to light and dark wallpapers.

05

All apps

A clean grid of everything installed. Hold OK to pin or unpin from the dock.

06

Made for TV remotes

Every control works with the arrow keys, OK and Back. No touch needed.

Install

Three steps.

1

Download

Download the APK file to a USB drive or to your phone.

2

Install on the TV

Open the file on the TV with a file manager and allow installs from unknown sources. Or install over Wi-Fi with adb:

adb connect TV-IP:5555
adb install sambhav-tv-os.apk
3

Set as home

Optional. To use it as your home screen, run:

adb shell cmd package \
 set-home-activity \
